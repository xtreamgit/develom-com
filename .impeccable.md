## Target Audience

CTOs, Compliance Officers, IT Directors, VP Engineering, and Operations Leaders at mid-size companies (50–500 employees) in **healthcare, financial services, insurance, and legal**. They are:

- Analytical and risk-aware — they need evidence, not enthusiasm
- Skeptical of AI vendors — they have been burned by overpromised tools
- Time-constrained — they scan before they read
- Accountable — a bad AI vendor choice costs them their job

They are NOT: startup founders, developers, or early adopters looking for the next shiny thing.

---

## Use Cases

1. **Evaluate credibility** — Is Develom a real firm that understands regulated industries?
2. **Understand the offer** — What exactly does Develom build, and for whom?
3. **Check compliance posture** — Do they understand HIPAA, AML, GDPR, SOC 2?
4. **Book a discovery call** — The one and only conversion goal
5. **Read thought leadership** — Blog posts that demonstrate deep domain expertise

---

## Brand Personality

**Serious authority with quiet confidence.**

Think: the difference between a generic SaaS landing page and a boutique professional firm that has clearly done this many times before. The site should feel like the work of people who are deeply competent and have nothing to prove.

- **Tone:** Direct. Substantive. No fluff. No hype. No exclamation marks.
- **Feel:** Premium without being flashy. Dense with meaning, not with decoration.
- **Trust signals:** Specificity over vagueness. "HIPAA § 164.312(a)" beats "enterprise-grade security."

## Style Direction

**Target aesthetic:** Minimalist Professional with deliberate Bento Grid influence on portfolio and services sections.

- Clean whitespace — sections breathe, nothing feels cramped
- Navy backgrounds for hero and CTA sections — electric blue for all interactive elements
- Cards: subtle `ring-1 ring-slate-200` border, `shadow-sm`, `rounded-lg` — never rounded-full
- Icons: line-style SVG only — no emoji, no filled cartoon icons
- Data and numbers get special treatment — larger, navy or electric blue, DM Mono

---

## Anti-Patterns — Never Use

These make the site look like every other AI company. Avoid completely:

- Purple or violet as a primary or accent color
- Gradient hero backgrounds (purple→blue, pink→purple, etc.)
- Animated particle or mesh backgrounds
- Floating geometric shapes or abstract "AI brain" illustrations
- Stock photo people (smiling diverse team on a laptop)
- Hollow oversized icons as section decorators
- "Transform your business" or "Unlock the power of AI" headline copy
- Rocket, lightning bolt, or sparkle emoji in UI
- Cards with `rounded-2xl` or larger on desktop layouts
- Glassmorphism on primary content surfaces

## Product Type

**Professional services website** for a boutique AI automation firm. Closest reference points:

- McKinsey.com (authority, restraint)
- Palantir.com (technical credibility, dark surfaces)
- Linear.app (precision, clean interaction design)

Not: Vercel.com, Stripe.com, or any consumer SaaS — those skew too playful for this audience.
